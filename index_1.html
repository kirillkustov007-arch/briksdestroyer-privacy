Bricks Destroyer: Privacy Policy

App: Bricks Destroyer: Ball Breaker (Android, package com.DFS.BriksDestroyer)
Developer: DFS
Effective date: October 14, 2026

This policy explains what information the Android version of Bricks Destroyer (the “Game”), distributed on Google Play, collects, how it is used and what choices you have. The Game has no user accounts and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

1. Data stored only on your device

Your level progress, stars, gems (crystals), lives, bonuses, settings (sound, vibration), daily rewards and quests, your purchase records and your ad-consent choice are saved locally on your device. We do not receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

3. Advertising and your choices

The Game is free and is supported by ads. You can buy the “No Ads” pack to remove full-screen ads; rewarded videos remain optional.

  • EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland: on first launch the Game asks for your consent before the ad service starts. If you tap Accept, our partners may use your advertising ID to show personalised ads and to measure ads, and Firebase may use it for analytics. If you tap Decline, ads are shown without personalisation and Firebase works in a restricted mode. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad Privacy.
  • Everywhere: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on your device).

4. Legal bases (EEA / UK)

We rely on your consent for personalised advertising and for using your advertising ID; on our legitimate interest in providing, securing and improving the Game for non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and basic analytics; and on performance of a contract to deliver in-app purchases you make.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data retention

We do not keep personal data on our own servers. Analytics data is kept by Google for a limited period set in our Firebase settings (no longer than 14 months for user-level data). Ad providers keep data according to their own policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Because the Game has no accounts, most data is held by the services listed above, and we can only act on data we can link to your device. Send requests to the contact address below; we may ask for information that helps us find the data (for example the date you installed the Game). You can also contact Unity and Google directly.

Deleting your data: uninstall the Game to delete all local data, reset your advertising ID in Android settings, and contact us to request deletion of analytics data.

8. Security

Data sent to the services above is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game changes. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
