Who we are

Briks Destroyer is a casual brick-breaking game made by the independent developer DFS ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is handled when you play the game on Android and how you can control it.

Information stored on your device

Your level progress, crystals, lives, bonuses, settings and purchase records are saved locally on your device. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it.

Advertising

The game is free and supported by ads: optional rewarded videos (for example, to get an extra life) and full-screen ads between levels. Ads are delivered by these third-party services:

  • Unity LevelPlay (ad mediation) — privacy policy
  • Unity Ads (ad network) — privacy policy

To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, these services may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID);
  • device information such as model, operating system version, language and screen size;
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • information about how you interact with ads (views, clicks, completions).

This data is processed by the providers under their own privacy policies. We only receive aggregated reports, such as the number of ads shown.

Your choices: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your device). You can still play the game; ads may become less relevant.

In-app purchases

You can buy optional items, such as crystals or removing ads between levels. Payments are processed by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase was completed, so the game can deliver the item. We do not receive your name, email or payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and deletion

We do not keep any personal data on our own servers. Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the game. For data held by the advertising providers, you can reset your advertising ID as described above or use the request options in their privacy policies. You can also email us and we will help with your request.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. Because we do not store personal data ourselves, most requests are handled by the advertising providers listed above; we will gladly help you contact them.

Security

Data exchanged with the advertising and payment services is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated.
